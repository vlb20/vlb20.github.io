I am a **PhD student** in the [SEART](https://seart.si.usi.ch) research group at the [Software Institute](https://si.usi.ch), [USI Lugano](https://www.usi.ch/en), supervised by [Prof. Gabriele Bavota](https://www.inf.usi.ch/faculty/bavota/) and co-supervised by [Dr. Rosalia Tufano](https://www.inf.usi.ch/phd/tufanr/). My research focuses on <span class="pill">AI for Software Engineering</span>.

Previously, I completed my Master's degree in **Computer Engineering** at the University of Naples Federico II with 110/110 cum laude, working on ontology-driven hazard generation for autonomous driving systems under the supervision of [Prof. Roberto Pietrantuono](http://wpage.unina.it/roberto.pietrantuono/index.html).
